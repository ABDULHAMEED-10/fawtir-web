## Your first invoice in <em>one request</em>

Your ERP sends its own invoice JSON. A mapping your company certifies turns it into a PINT-OM e-invoice. The examples use fictitious data.

<Split>
  <div slot="text">
    <Steps>
      1. **Certify a mapping** for your ERP's invoice data in the Fawtir web app.
      2. **Create an `ERP_INGESTION` key.** It can send documents and read nothing.
      3. **Send the invoice** with one `POST`. A valid document is answered `202 Accepted`.
      4. **Read its status** with an OAuth client that holds only `invoice.read`.
    </Steps>
    [Follow the quick start](/get-started/quick-start/)

## Three ways <em>to connect</em>

All three use the same certified mapping and the same validation, and produce the same PINT-OM document.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="API Push" icon="rocket">
    Your ERP calls Fawtir's REST API, one document per request, with an `ERP_INGESTION` key. Invoices, credit notes, debit notes and self-billed documents. The usual choice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Managed Pull" icon="seti:pipeline">
    Fawtir calls your ERP's HTTPS JSON endpoint on a schedule and collects new tax invoices with a cursor. For ERPs that already expose invoices over REST.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bridge Upload" icon="document">
    A person signed in to Fawtir uploads one exported invoice, as JSON or XML, up to 4 MiB. For low volume, a pilot, or recovering a single document.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What Fawtir does" icon="approve-check">
    Converts with your mapping, validates in five layers, builds the PINT-OM e-invoice, delivers it over Peppol AS4 and reports the Tax Data Document.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
